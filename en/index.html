Want different ringtones for each SIM on Pixel 9a?

With dual SIM, it is useful to know whether a work or personal number is ringing without checking the screen.

Pixel 9a does not provide a standard setting that assigns a separate ringtone to SIM 1 and SIM 2.

Dual Ringtone was built to solve exactly that problem.

What Dual Ringtone does

Detects SIM 1 or SIM 2

The app identifies which SIM received the incoming call and plays the ringtone assigned to it.

Separate MP3 files

Select one MP3 for SIM 1 and another MP3 for SIM 2.

Built for Pixel 9a

Pixel 9a is the defined development and test device rather than claiming broad compatibility.

Setup

  1. Set the standard Pixel ringtone to “My Sounds → None.”
  2. Select an MP3 for SIM 1 in Dual Ringtone.
  3. Select another MP3 for SIM 2.
  4. The app monitors incoming calls and plays the MP3 assigned to the receiving SIM.

FAQ

Does it work on phones other than Pixel 9a?

Google Pixel 9a is currently the officially supported and tested device.

Can SIM 1 and SIM 2 use different songs?

Yes. You select an MP3 separately for SIM 1 and SIM 2.

Why set the standard Pixel ringtone to None?

It prevents the standard Pixel ringtone and the Dual Ringtone MP3 from playing at the same time.

Does the app collect personal data?

The app uses no ads, Firebase, Crashlytics, or analytics and does not collect personal information.
