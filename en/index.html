Dual Ringtone is an Android app for Google Pixel 9a that lets you assign a different MP3 ringtone to SIM 1 and SIM 2. Know whether a work or personal number is ringing before you look at the screen.

Supported device: Google Pixel 9a. Google Play closed beta testers are currently wanted.

A practical Pixel dual SIM ringtone solution

Pixel 9a does not provide a standard setting for assigning a separate ringtone to each SIM. Dual Ringtone detects which SIM receives the call and plays the MP3 selected for that SIM.

Built for Pixel 9a

How it works

  1. Set the standard Pixel ringtone to “My Sounds → None.”
  2. Select an MP3 ringtone for SIM 1 in Dual Ringtone.
  3. Select another MP3 ringtone for SIM 2.
  4. The app monitors incoming calls and plays the ringtone assigned to the receiving SIM.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work on phones other than Pixel 9a?

Google Pixel 9a is currently the only officially supported and tested device.

Does the app collect personal information?

No. The app uses no ads, Firebase, Crashlytics, or analytics, and does not collect personal information.

Why must the standard Pixel ringtone be set to None?

This prevents the standard ringtone and the Dual Ringtone MP3 from playing at the same time.
