Google Play Closed Testing

Pixel 9a
closed testers wanted

We are looking for Google Pixel 9a users with dual SIM enabled to test separate ringtones for SIM 1 and SIM 2 in everyday use.

Limited to 10 testers — first come, first served.

Who can participate

Google Pixel 9a

This is the current official test device.

Dual SIM enabled

You can receive and test incoming calls on both SIM 1 and SIM 2.

Google Play account

A Google account is required to join the closed test.

At least two weeks of testing

After joining, keep Dual Ringtone installed for at least two weeks and use it normally while checking incoming-call behavior.

What we would like you to test

  1. A call to SIM 1 plays the MP3 ringtone selected for SIM 1.
  2. A call to SIM 2 plays the MP3 ringtone selected for SIM 2.
  3. Monitoring resumes correctly after restarting the Pixel 9a.
  4. Ringtone looping and stopping when a call is answered or ends work naturally.
If something does not work, it helps if you tell us whether it involved SIM 1 or SIM 2, whether it happened after a reboot, and when the issue occurred.

Closed Tester Benefit

We plan to let participating testers continue using Dual Ringtone at no charge after the official release

If you complete at least two weeks of testing, we plan to provide a way for you to continue using Dual Ringtone at no charge after the official release.

Limited to 10 testers — first come, first served.

The exact no-charge distribution method will be announced after confirming the appropriate Google Play method.

Closed testing FAQ

What does “at least two weeks” mean?

Keep Dual Ringtone installed for at least two weeks after joining and use it normally while checking calls to SIM 1 and SIM 2.

Can I participate with a phone other than Pixel 9a?

Google Pixel 9a is currently the officially supported closed-test device.

Can I keep using the app after the test?

If you complete at least two weeks of testing, we plan to provide a way for you to continue using Dual Ringtone at no charge after the official release. This benefit is limited to 10 testers on a first-come, first-served basis.
