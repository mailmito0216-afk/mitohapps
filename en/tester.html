Google Play Closed Testing

Become a Dual Ringtone
closed tester

We are looking for dual-SIM Google Pixel 9a users to help test real incoming calls.

Who can participate

Google Pixel 9a

Pixel 9a is the current test device.

Dual SIM enabled

You can receive test calls on both SIM 1 and SIM 2.

Google Play account

A Google account is required to join the closed test.

How to join

  1. Register using the tester application button below.
  2. Open the Google Play opt-in page with the same registered Google account.
  3. Select “Become a tester,” then install the app from Google Play.
  4. Test calls to SIM 1 and SIM 2 and send us your results or comments.

Participation links

Before publishing, replace these two placeholders with the actual URLs.

TESTER_APPLICATION_URL: Google Form or email registration

GOOGLE_PLAY_OPT_IN_URL: Google Play closed-test opt-in URL

Apply to become a tester Open Google Play opt-in page

What we would like you to test

Incoming calls

SIM 1 plays the SIM 1 MP3, and SIM 2 plays the SIM 2 MP3.

After reboot

Ringtone monitoring and SIM-specific playback still work after restarting the Pixel 9a.

Volume, looping, and stopping

The ringtone volume is appropriate, loops while ringing, and stops when the call is answered or ends.

Please note

This is a test version and unexpected behavior is possible. Test it when a missed or affected call would not cause a serious problem.
